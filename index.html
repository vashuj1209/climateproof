Evidence, with boundaries.

Flags use a statistical median absolute deviation baseline (threshold 3.5, minimum 5 readings per sensor), not a trained AI model. A flag is a prompt for investigation, not proof of an error. Repeated values can make deviations stand out strongly.

Receipts are local files, not blockchain anchors or trusted timestamps. A matching fingerprint shows unchanged bytes relative to that receipt; it does not prove sensor accuracy or protect against replacement of both file and receipt. No measured emissions-reduction claims are made.
